About

Zheng (Bella) Chen is a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Bo Li (Sep 2024 – Dec 2027). She received her Bachelor of Science degree with a minor in Finance from The University of Hong Kong (2020–2024).

Her research focuses on Trustworthy and Controllable Large Language Models, with particular interests in AI Agents, Large Reasoning Models, Explainable Machine Learning, and foundations-driven evaluation of activation steering methods. She aims to build multi-level, downstream-task-grounded benchmarks that rigorously assess effectiveness, side effects, robustness, and cross-model reliability.

Research Themes

LLM Steering & Controllability

Foundations-oriented evaluation of activation steering across models, locations, and methods.

Reasoning & Reliability

Understanding and mitigating sycophancy, hallucinations, and logical inconsistencies in LLMs.

Usable Explainable AI & Agents

Transparent, controllable interventions for safer real-world deployment of AI systems.
